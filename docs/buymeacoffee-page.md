**About / "What are you creating?"**
I build small, free tools like Cache Companions, a little animal for Claude Code that keeps your prompt cache warm while you're in meetings and saves you money. Away from the keyboard I rescue animals: I put out food for stray and feral cats, get them healthy, foster them, and find them homes, or a working life on a farm for the ones who'll never be lap cats. I've rescued more than 20 cats so far, all self-funded. Every coffee goes to food, vet care and fostering.
